# Put Your Venue Inside Tonight's Move

## What YourNotDown Is
YourNotDown helps visitors and locals decide what to do tonight in just a few taps.

A guest picks a vibe, then gets a curated Tonight's Move itinerary built around categories like Dinner, Drinks, Live Music, and Late Night.

For the right venue, that means showing up at the exact moment someone is deciding where to go next.

## Why Sponsors Care
- Shows up at decision time, not after the choice has already been made
- Placement is matched to the guest's selected vibe and itinerary category
- Built for real nightlife intent, not passive impressions
- Designed for restaurants, cocktail bars, rooftops, live music venues, late-night spots, and standout local experiences
- Feels like a curated local recommendation, not a generic ad placement

## How The Weighting Works
Sponsored venues receive additional recommendation weight when they are a fit for the guest's selected vibe and itinerary category.

Current weighting:
- Normal: 1x
- Silver: 5x
- Gold: 10x
- Platinum: 20x

In plain English: if two venues are similarly relevant for the same guest and slot, the sponsor-tier venue has stronger odds of appearing.

## Example Odds
In a simple example with 20 eligible venues and equal relevance:

- One Silver sponsor + 19 non-sponsors: Silver appears about **20.83%** of the time
- One Gold sponsor + 19 non-sponsors: Gold appears about **34.48%** of the time
- One Platinum sponsor + 19 non-sponsors: Platinum appears about **51.28%** of the time

These are example odds only, shown to illustrate how weighting works.
