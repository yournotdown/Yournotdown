## You're Not Down

You're Not Down is Blake's nightlife and tourist discovery product.

Core product:
- Help visitors quickly decide what to do tonight.
- Nashville first.
- Hotel QR code distribution.
- Simple itinerary flow.
- 1 to 4 step plans.
- Dark playful neon-lime emoji-forward brand.
- Built for tourists, hotels, bars, restaurants, and local activity partners.

Product guardrails:
- This is not a bloated social app.
- This is not a complex account-heavy platform.
- The main job is to help someone quickly find a good move tonight.
- Do not overbuild accounts, feeds, DMs, followers, or unnecessary social features.
- Prefer simple save/magic-link flows over complex login systems.
- Preserve the dark/neon/fun brand.
- Keep hotel and tourist flows simple.

Safety rules:
- Never deploy without Blake's explicit approval.
- Never merge to main without Blake's explicit approval.
- Never delete listings, partner data, venue data, env vars, secrets, production settings, or branches without Blake's explicit approval.

Working rules:
- Before starting work, read `AGENTS.md`, `PROJECT_STATUS.md`, `NEXT_ACTIONS.md`, `DECISIONS.md`, and `RUNBOOK.md`, then run `git status` and summarize branch, dirty files, project state, and safest next step.
- Do not edit files until Blake approves the next task.
